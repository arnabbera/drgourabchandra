About the doctor

Trusted orthopedic expertise, centred around you.

Dr. Gourab Chandra is an experienced orthopedic surgeon based in Kolkata. He provides focused care for trauma, fractures, joint disorders and other orthopedic conditions, helping patients regain strength and movement.

He completed his MBBS in 2013 and MS in Orthopaedics in 2017 from BN Mandal University. His approach combines clinical precision with clear, reassuring communication.
